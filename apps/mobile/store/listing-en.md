## Full description
DogVantage is your digital dog trainer. The app builds a personal weekly plan from breed, age, and your goals — and adapts as you log sessions.

**What’s included**
- Today’s exercises on Home, with clear reps and guides
- Per-rep success/fail logging and session summaries
- Calendar history of trained days
- Skills and learning guides for structured progression
- AI chat for Pro: practical advice grounded in your dog and training history

**Approach**
Training is based on positive reinforcement (R+) and measurable criteria — not punishment. Content is training guidance, not veterinary care. For health or serious behaviour concerns, consult a veterinarian or qualified behaviour professional.

**Account & subscription**
Sign up with email. Subscriptions are managed on dogvantage.se (not via in-app purchase). You can delete your account anytime in Profile.

**Privacy**
https://dogvantage.se/privacy
